Foundation metadata models, locator, scanner, formatters, inspector, logging and POSIX helpers were vendored from the owner's iclouddoctor/Packages/SyncDoctorCore on 2026-09-09. This module has no sibling-checkout dependency, separate entitlement system or private iCloud API. ClearDisk owns licensing and history persistence.

Scanner is metadata-only, caps results at 50,000 items and event buffering at 64, and reports partial coverage. scanToResult propagates cancellation to its worker and returns the collected partial result. Thread-local materialization policy is restored after synchronous work. It never changes the process-wide policy.

Archive writes a unique private staging folder and a completion manifest only after SHA-256 byte verification and repeated source metadata/membership checks under NSFileCoordinator. It never deletes an original or a failed staging copy. Public entry only accepts iCloud Drive sources and a local ~/ClearDisk Archives destination. It refuses symlinks, aliases, resource-fork files, executable files, semantic/unknown extended attributes, special files, dataless items, unknown/unsafe cloud metadata and excessive member counts. Downloads require a separate explicit action. Archives support data-only document packages and regular files; application/executable bundles and semantic metadata are refused with Finder guidance. They preserve data-fork bytes and directory membership; they do not preserve ACLs, ownership or provider bookkeeping attributes. This is disclosed in the manifest. The dedicated destination excludes known iCloud/FileProvider locations but cannot detect every third-party client configured to watch arbitrary home folders. External writers that ignore file coordination cannot be guaranteed to remain stable after verification. Directory/package support does not imply application-consistent snapshots of open databases: close the owning app first.
