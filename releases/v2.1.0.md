ClearDisk 2.1.0 adds two read-only tools under **Mac checks**. A full disk scan is not required.

- **Apple Intelligence storage:** inspect recognized model and support folders, their measured sizes, and locations that cannot be read. macOS manages these assets; ClearDisk does not delete them. Protected files can leave a partial result.
- **Intel app review:** identify Intel-only main executables in your Applications folders, with separate Universal, Apple silicon and undetermined results. Plug-ins, extensions and helper programs need a separate review.

Requires macOS 15 or later. Universal Apple silicon + Intel app, Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple. Existing licenses continue to work; scanning remains free and cleanup remains a $10 one-time license.

**Update:** On ClearDisk 2.0.0, choose ClearDisk → Check for Updates. On 1.1.1 or earlier, download the installer manually once.
