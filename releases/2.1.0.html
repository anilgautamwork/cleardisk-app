ClearDisk 2.1.0

Two new read-only checks, built into ClearDisk.

  • Apple Intelligence storage: measure recognized model and support folders, see where they live, and understand which locations could not be read. macOS manages these assets; ClearDisk does not delete them.
  • Intel app review: find installed apps with Intel-only main executables before upgrading beyond macOS 27. Universal, Apple silicon and undetermined results stay distinct. Plug-ins and helper programs need a separate review.

Open Mac checks in the sidebar. A full disk scan is not required. Neither check changes your files.

Read Apple's Rosetta compatibility guidance, including the limited exception for certain older games.
