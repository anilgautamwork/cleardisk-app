ClearDisk 1.1.0 for macOS 15 and later. Universal Apple silicon and Intel app.

iCloud Doctor is now part of ClearDisk:

- Scan accessible iCloud Drive metadata without opening file contents or requesting downloads during a scan.
- Inspect upload/download errors, conflicts, pending transfers, cloud-only items and local storage usage. Missing metadata is shown as unknown.
- Search and filter results, inspect raw metadata and compare pending observations across scans. A potentially stuck transfer is an observation, not proof of an Apple service failure.
- Explicitly request downloads, or remove eligible local downloads after confirmation. Removing a local download affects Mac storage, not iCloud account capacity.
- Archive downloaded files or folders to a verified local copy without overwriting existing files. Cloud originals are preserved; review them in Finder separately.

Scanning remains free. Existing ClearDisk licenses unlock the licensed actions; there is no second iCloud Doctor subscription. Keep Downloaded, account quota, Photos/Notes/Messages and server-side repair remain Apple-managed.
