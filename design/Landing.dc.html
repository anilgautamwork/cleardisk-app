Questions people actually ask

What is System Data on Mac, exactly?
It's macOS's catch-all bucket: app caches, logs, Time Machine snapshots, iPhone backups, temporary working files and leftovers from old apps. Apple shows you the total but not the contents — that's the part ClearDisk opens up.
Is it safe? I've heard cleaner apps break things.
ClearDisk is notarized by Apple and removals go to your Trash by default, where you can undo them. System-critical files are locked out entirely, and items are labeled honestly: Safe, Review, or Leave it alone.
Why is my System Data 100+ GB?
Usually a handful of big culprits: local Time Machine snapshots, forgotten iPhone backups, developer tools, or a runaway cache. Run the free scan — it tells you your exact answer in about a minute.
Does my data leave my Mac?
Never. ClearDisk has no servers, no accounts, no analytics on your files. Everything happens on your Mac.
