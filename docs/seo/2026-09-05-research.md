# ClearDisk organic search research — 5 September 2026

## Decision

Keep **cleardisk.app**, ClearDisk, and the existing ThreeUI design. Build a connected library of useful Mac-storage guides around System Data. Prioritize problem resolution and free-scan downloads; measure purchases downstream. A one-page site can rank, but separate pages let us answer distinct questions well. Page count alone is not a ranking advantage.

## Evidence quality

The accompanying keyword-map.csv contains **91 candidate phrases**, grouped into 14 intent clusters including exclusions. It is a prioritization map, not every search or a paid-tool export. P1/P2 are editorial priorities based on product fit and observed problems, not measured ranking difficulty. Phrase variants are candidates unless their specific source establishes them.

- **Historical estimate:** Ahrefs' public Macworld page lists “how to clear cache on mac” at 14,000 US monthly searches. The page is dated **July 2025**, so the chat's presentation of it as current demand is too strong. This is a third-party estimate, not our Search Console data. [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/websites/macworld.com).
- **Current qualitative evidence:** Apple maintains storage-full guidance and documents System Data. An Apple Community user describes storage growing again after attempted cache cleanup. These establish problems and vocabulary, not monthly volumes or conversion rates. [Apple storage guidance](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102624), [System Data definition](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/mac-help/mchl3d437fbc/mac), [recurring-growth report](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/256249051).
- **Competition exists even in long tails:** results include dedicated utilities, Apple, MacPaw and publishers. “Least competition”, “easy rankings”, and the specific Tahoe bug spike in the old plan are unverified.
- **Unverified:** old cluster totals (25–50K System Data, 70–130K cache, etc.), exact long-tail volumes, CPC and organic KD. Missing values stay empty, not zero. Do not sum overlapping close variants.
- **Google Ads competition is advertiser competition**, not organic keyword difficulty. Monthly searches depend on period, geography and network and include close variants. [Google metric definitions](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3022575).

Default market: English-language US, matching earlier research. The English product can serve other countries; do not multiply US volumes into a global estimate.

## Content and conversion

Answer before pitching. Each guide supplies a diagnostic sequence, destructive consequences, relevant Apple references and two or three useful internal links. Name limitations that affect the reader's decision. ClearDisk is publisher; do not invent a human reviewer, credentials, customers, tests or recovered-space results.

Use app source as original evidence: named buckets, protected folders, allocated file sizes, Trash-first removal and Full Disk Access boundaries. Example figures are not visitor scans. The current download is 0.1.0, not the planned licensed 1.0 release.

Funnel: **search → helpful guide → free download → local scan → informed cleanup purchase**. “See what's using your space” links to /download; secondary pricing explains $10 once at launch. Guides use a readable narrow column, clear headings and native links, without loading the homepage shader or large demo.

System Data is a changing catch-all, not all junk. Moving files to Trash does not release their space until Trash is emptied. Local Time Machine snapshots are managed by macOS and can count as available storage; their displayed size is not automatically additional reclaimable space. [Apple category reference](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/mac-help/mchl3d437fbc/mac), [Trash guidance](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102624), [snapshots](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/102154).

## Technical SEO

- Meaningful initial HTML on every public route, one H1, unique title/description, self-canonical and descriptive crawlable links. [Google JavaScript guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/javascript-seo-basics).
- Sitemap and guide links derive from one published-content registry. Only canonical indexable 200 routes belong in it. Real 404 for unknown pages; real editorial dates, never build-time dates disguised as updates. [Google sitemap guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap).
- Article and BreadcrumbList structured data match visible content; no fabricated ratings/authors. FAQs remain useful for readers, but Google retired FAQ rich results in May 2026. llms.txt is not needed for Google Search. [Google updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates).
- Explicit preview noindex. Production indexing is deliberately enabled on https://cleardisk.app with self-canonicals and redirects for controlled alternate hosts. Transactional pages and APIs stay out of the sitemap; noindex/no-store where appropriate. robots.txt is not access control; do not block crawling where a noindex directive must be read.
- Useful original content matters more than generated page volume. No near-duplicate doorway pages or keyword stuffing. [Helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content), [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).
